Deleting Your Data

Mathdoku · published by zowle
Requests are answered within 30 days

The short version. Mathdoku keeps two kinds of data. Almost all of it — your puzzle, your notes, your timer, your scores, your settings — is stored on your device only, and you delete it yourself in one step, without writing to anybody. The only thing that lives on a server is your entry on the online leaderboard; that one we delete for you, within 30 days of your request.

1. The data on your device

The following is written to the private storage of the app and never uploaded: your current score and your last submitted score, the puzzle you are working on with the numbers you entered, your notes and the time spent on it, the grid size, the operators and the difficulty you picked, your colour scheme, your language, and your best time, best score and best stars for every combination of grid size and operators.

You delete all of it yourself:

  • on Android, Settings → Apps → Mathdoku → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the app;
  • on iOS, Settings → General → iPhone Storage → Mathdoku → Delete App;
  • if you use Android backup, remove the backup of the app in the backup settings of the system as well — the app keeps its preferences out of the backup, so there is nothing of yours in it, but an old backup can be removed there in any case.

There is no copy of that data anywhere else, so nothing has to be deleted on a server afterwards.

2. The data on the leaderboard

The online leaderboard holds one record per player, made of four fields:

  • an identifier — your advertising identifier, or the local identifier the app made for itself when there is no advertising identifier;
  • your score;
  • your nickname;
  • your country or region.

Your entry is removed on request. Write to pangkunyi@gmail.com and tell us which entry to delete.

What the request has to contain

The nickname and the score shown next to it on the leaderboard — your best score is fine. The nickname on its own is not enough, because nicknames are not unique: several players can be called the same thing, and without the score we cannot tell which of them you mean. A request that names the nickname and the score of the entry is enough for us to find it.

An example:

Please delete my entry on the Mathdoku leaderboard.
Nickname: PuzzleFan
Score: 1284

What happens after you ask

We remove the entry from the leaderboard within 30 days of your request and answer the same address by email to confirm it. Until then the entry stays visible, because it is part of a public list.

3. Advertising data

The ads of Mathdoku are served by Google AdMob, and Google handles that data under its own privacy policy. You are in control of it: you can reset your advertising identifier, delete it, delete the advertising data of Google in the settings of your device, or turn off ad personalisation. The app itself falls back to a local identifier when no advertising identifier is available.
