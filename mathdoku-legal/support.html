Mathdoku — published by zowle

Mathdoku is an arithmetic puzzle: fill every cage of the grid so that its numbers match the arithmetic clue, using + − × ÷. The puzzle itself is a completely offline game — no account, no sign-in, no email address. The game is free; it shows ads and it has an online leaderboard for the hundred best players.

Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. Mathdoku has no account system and never asks you to sign in or to type an email address. Your nickname and the country you pick in Settings are what label your entry on the leaderboard; everything else stays on your device.

My progress is gone. What happened?

Progress is stored on your device only. Uninstalling the app, clearing its storage, or using the system's "clear app data" function removes it, and it cannot be recovered afterwards — there is no server copy of your puzzles. The app also keeps its preferences out of the Android backup, so a restored backup does not bring your scores back either.

Does the puzzle keep going when I close the app?

Yes. The puzzle you are working on, the time you have spent on it and the notes you wrote in the cells are all saved, and they are there when you come back — even after the app was closed or the device was switched off.

Can I undo a mistake?

Yes. Undo and redo go back up to 100 steps, so a wrong number, a wrong note or an accidental erase can be taken back.

How large are the grids?

From 3×3 up to 9×9. Every size has its own record, so your best time and your best score are kept per size and per set of operators. You can also switch the operators on or off: addition, subtraction, multiplication or division.

Can I change the language?

Yes. Open Settings → Language and pick one of the 22 languages the app ships, or leave it following your device language. On the first start the app follows the language of the device. On Android 13 and later, your system settings also has a per-app language picker that writes the same choice.

How do the colour schemes work?

Settings → Theme offers five schemes — classic, forest, violet, sky and night — plus one that follows the dark mode of your device. The scheme follows the device until you pick one of your own, and the game switches over as soon as the device changes its mode.

What is on the leaderboard?

The hundred best players, with the nickname, the score and the country or region next to each name. Your own row is highlighted and the list scrolls to it, so you can see your place at once. The list can be pulled down to refresh it.

Can I print puzzles?

Yes. The print button in the bottom bar builds a sheet of twelve puzzles and hands it to the system print dialog, where you can send it to a printer or save it as a PDF. The share button next to it opens the system share sheet. Both work entirely on your device: the sheet is never uploaded to a server.

Why do I see ads?

The game is free. A banner ad sits at the bottom of the puzzle screen while you play. There is one more ad, and it only comes when you ask for it: the button with the arrow in the top row watches a short full-screen ad and the game fills one correct number into the board for you as a hint, once a minute. The game never shows such an ad on its own — there is no ad when you finish a puzzle. The ads are served by Google AdMob, and you can reset or delete your advertising identifier, or turn off ad personalisation, in the settings of your device.

Does the app send anything anywhere?

Two things: your leaderboard entry, and the request for an ad. Everything else — your puzzle, your notes, your timer, your settings — stays on your device. The privacy policy has the details, field by field.
